1. Introduction

Welcome to Al-Bukhari ("the App"), developed by Taha Imam. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Data Collection & Offline Functionality

The App is primarily an offline Hadith reading tool. We do not require account registration, login credentials, or personal identity details (such as your name, phone number, or email address) to browse the Hadith collection.

All personal app preferences—including your bookmarks, custom notes, reading progress, and theme choices—are stored strictly on your local device. We do not upload your personal notes or bookmarks to any external server.

3. Internet Usage & Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The core Hadith reading feature works completely offline. However, the App connects to the internet solely to serve advertisements via Google AdMob.

Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) may automatically collect and process certain non-personal device information to deliver and measure advertisements, including:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID) for ad personalization, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.
  • Coarse Location Data: Approximate IP-based location to serve regionally relevant ads.
  • App Performance & Diagnostics: Anonymous crash logs and ad load metrics.

For detailed information on how Google manages advertising data, please refer to Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms.
